How Simulation Works
01 · MODIFY
Sliders adjust raw indicator values in the original dataset before re-processing.
02 · RE-SCALE
MinMaxScaler normalises the modified data. The weighted stability index is recomputed.
03 · PREDICT
The trained Random Forest model predicts stability on the modified data.
04 · COMPARE
Delta (Δ) shows the projected improvement or decline vs. the unmodified baseline.
